## **PART A — BRANDING MASTERPLAN**

### **1) North Star & Objectives**

**North Star**: Flow keeps your money moving with ease—simple, fast, and human.

**12‑month Goals (Realistic & Honest)**:
- **People Actually Know We Exist**: 10% unaided recall in BD urban Gen‑Z/Young adult target group
- **People Actually Use It**: 35% Day 7 retention, 18% Day 30 retention  
- **People Actually Like It**: NPS ≥ 45, 20% referral‑led signups
- **People Actually Pay**: $25K ARR by end of Year 1 (focus on product-market fit)

### **2) What We've Learned (The Hard Way)**

**The Reality Check**: Most finance apps treat money like a math problem. But money is emotional, cultural, and deeply personal.

- **Trust Deficit**: People don't trust apps with their money data, especially in Bangladesh
- **The 10-Second Rule**: If adding an expense takes longer than buying the thing, people won't do it
- **Numeral Recognition**: ১,০০,০০০ is instantly readable to Bengali speakers, 100,000 requires mental translation
- **Connectivity Reality**: Internet isn't always there—offline-first isn't optional, it's essential
- **Payment Reality**: bKash isn't just a payment method in BD, it's how people actually pay for things

### **3) Competitive Map (Honest Assessment)**

**Global Trackers**: Generic UX, English-first, online-dependent, built for Silicon Valley budgets
**Local BD Apps**: Language support exists but feels dated, slow UX, no cultural intelligence
**Bank/Wallet Apps**: Locked into specific ecosystems, category-specific, not personal finance

**Flow's Edge**: Speed (≤9s) + Indic numerals + Offline-first + Gentle habit formation + Export-friendly

### **4) Positioning & Value Prop**

**Positioning Statement**: Flow is the Bengali-first Personal Finance OS that makes daily money decisions effortless—ultra‑fast entry, crystal‑clear totals in your own numerals, and privacy you can feel.

**Category**: Personal Finance OS  
**For**: Freelancers, students/young adults, small families who are tired of feeling stupid about money  
**Unlike**: slow, English-first, online-dependent trackers that make you feel judged  
**Flow delivers**: Calm • Clear • Fast money management

### **5) Audience Personas (Real People, Real Struggles)**

#### **Arif (22) — Student/Part‑timer**
**The Reality**: "আমি জানি আমার চা-নাশতায় অনেক টাকা যায়, কিন্তু লিখে রাখতে ভালো লাগে না। খুব বোরিং।"
**How Flow Helps**: ২ ট্যাপে খরচ লিখে streak বজায় রাখে, দেখে মনে হয় কিছু একটা করছি

#### **Mitu (27) — Freelancer**  
**The Reality**: "My income changes every month, but my expenses don't. I need to see the full picture without doing math."
**How Flow Helps**: Multi-wallet view shows the real picture, budgets that adapt to irregular income

#### **Rahim & Tanjina (31/28) — Small family**
**The Reality**: "We tried budgeting apps before but they felt like someone was watching over our shoulder, judging our choices."
**How Flow Helps**: Shared view without judgment, gentle nudges instead of guilt trips

### **6) Brand Architecture**

- **Flow Core** — Track, categorize, budgets, insights
- **Flow Save** — Jars/goals, nudges  
- **Flow Pay** — Wallet awareness (bKash/Nagad mapping)
- **Flow Guard** — Privacy, backup/export, lock
- **Flow Plus** — Pro perks (CSV+, multi-wallet analytics, custom categories)

### **7) Message House**

**Roof (Tagline)**: Money in Motion, Life in Balance / টাকার flow, জীবনের balance

**Pillars**:
- **Speed**: keypad-first, recent chips, default wallet → ≤9s
- **Clarity**: Bengali numerals, clean cards, budget progress
- **Safety**: offline-first, explicit permissions, export-anytime  
- **Habit**: streaks, badges, gentle nudges

**Proof**: airplane-mode demo, timer overlay, privacy cards, public changelog

### **8) Go‑to‑Market (Realistic & Honest)**

**Phase 1: Start Small (Months 1-6)**
- Beta users: 1,000 people who actually use it daily
- University partnerships: Students who need expense tracking
- Authentic content: Real people, real stories, real struggles
- Community building: "Flow Fridays" wins wall

**Phase 2: Word of Mouth (Months 7-12)**
- Referral program: "Invite 3 friends, get 30 days Plus"
- User-generated content: Real screenshots, real testimonials
- Local influencers: People who actually understand the struggle

### **9) Success Metrics (Honest & Achievable)**

**Year 1 Goals**:
- 1,000+ active users who actually use it daily
- 60% onboarding completion (learning what confuses people)
- 45% Day 7 retention (figuring out what keeps people)
- $25K ARR (focus on product-market fit, not revenue)

**Year 2-4 Scaling**:
- 10,000+ users by Year 2
- $200K ARR by Year 2 (word-of-mouth growth)
- $800K ARR by Year 3 (cultural intelligence as moat)
- $2.5M ARR by Year 4 (platform maturity)

### **10) Risks & Mitigations (Real Talk)**

**"Flow" Generic Name**: Stylized ƒ mark + descriptor, legal TM on mark
**Privacy Doubts**: Offline demo, clear permissions, export-anytime, opt-in parsing
**Funnel Drop**: 9-second proof high in funnel, onboarding friction ≤ 2 screens before first add

## **PART B — BRAND BOOK**

### **1) Core Identity**

- **Brand**: Flow
- **Descriptor**: Personal Finance OS
- **Essence**: Calm • Clear • Fast
- **Primary Taglines**: 
  - EN — Money in Motion, Life in Balance
  - BN — টাকার flow, জীবনের balance
- **Secondary**: Your Pocket Accountant (legacy bridge)

### **2) Voice & Tone**

**Traits**: Mentor-friendly, judgment-free, concise, hopeful  
**Language**: Bengali-first with light English sprinkles  
**Do**: ছোট বাক্য, ক্রিয়া-কেন্দ্রিক, সাবলীল  
**Don't**: guilt, jargon, ফাঁপা প্রতিশ্রুতি

**Example Microcopy**:
- Empty: "আজ কিছু যোগ করা হয়নি। ১০ সেকেন্ডে একটা দিয়ে দেখি?"
- Success: "সেভ হলো—ব্যালেন্স আরও পরিষ্কার।"
- Budget alert: "এই ক্যাটাগরিতে ৮৭% ব্যবহার হয়েছে। প্ল্যান টিউন করবেন?"
- Offline: "ইন্টারনেট নেই—Flow কাজ করছে। সিঙ্ক পরে হবে।"
- Export: "এক্সপোর্ট রেডি। আপনার ডেটা, আপনার হাতে।"

### **3) Logo System**

- **Primary mark**: flowing ƒ (curved, continuous stroke) + wordmark Flow
- **Clear space**: 1× cap‑height around mark
- **Min sizes**: App icon 24px (Android/iOS), wordmark ≥ 96px width for web
- **Don'ts**: stretch/tilt, drop‑shadow clutter, low‑contrast over photos, gradient noise

### **4) Color Palette (WCAG‑aware)**

- **Trust Blue** #0F4C81 — Primary (AA on light backgrounds)
- **Mint/Teal** #22C3A6 — Accent (buttons, highlights)
- **Warm Sand** #F5F1EB — Background
- **Charcoal** #1F2937 — Text
- **Success** #16A34A
- **Warning** #F59E0B
- **Danger** #DC2626

### **5) Typography**

- **Latin**: Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Bengali**: Hind Siliguri / Noto Sans Bengali
- **Scale**: 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 32 / 40
- **Weights**: 400 / 500 / 600 / 700
- **Rules**: headlines bold, body 400/500, line‑height 1.4–1.6

### **6) Iconography & Illustration**

- **Icons**: rounded corners, 2px stroke, simple metaphors (money, jar, spark)
- **Illustration style**: flat‑minimal, warm neutrals, subtle textures
- **Photography**: real life, handheld POV, natural light, clutter‑free

### **7) Motion Principles**

- **Guideline**: Flow, don't flash
- **Micro‑interactions**: 150–250ms ease‑in‑out
- **Moments for delight**: first add success (tiny confetti), streak milestones, goal achieved
- **Reduce motion option**: respect OS setting

### **8) Layout & Components**

- **Grid**: 8pt base, 4pt nudge; mobile 4/8 gutters, 16–24 padding
- **Cards**: 12–16 radius, soft shadows, clear totals
- **Buttons**: primary filled (Mint), secondary outline (Blue), destructive (Danger)

### **9) Accessibility**

- **Text contrast**: ≥ AA, tap targets ≥ 44×44, meaningful labels (i18n), screen‑reader hints

### **10) Messaging Library (Plug‑and‑play)**

**Hooks**:
- BN: "খরচ লেখা কি ঝামেলা? Flow‑তে ১০ সেকেন্ডে শেষ।"
- EN: "If it takes longer than 10s, you won't do it."

**CTAs**:
- BN: "আজই Flow চেষ্টা করুন—একটা খরচ যোগ করুন।"
- EN: "Try Flow—add one expense, feel the calm."

**Trust Cards**:
- "Private‑by‑Design"
- "Offline‑First"  
- "Export Anytime"
- "Opt-in SMS Parsing (On‑device)"

### **11) Social & ASO Templates**

- **Reels**: stopwatch overlay (Tap → Type → Done ≤9s), airplane‑mode demo, streak wins
- **Tiles**: Indic numerals (১,০০,০০০ vs 100,000), budget progress bars
- **ASO**: 7 screenshots (BN/EN), short desc: Add in 9s. Offline‑first. Bengali numerals.

### **12) Legal & Privacy Copy Rules**

- **Never say**: "unbreakable/anonymous" unless true
- **Say**: "Private‑by‑Design, Offline-first, Export anytime"
- **SMS parsing**: explicit opt‑in, clear on‑device note
- **Link**: Privacy Policy, Data Deletion, Changelog

### **13) Analytics Event Taxonomy (min set)**

- expense_add_started, expense_add_completed (duration bucket)
- budget_created, budget_alert_seen
- export_started, export_completed
- streak_day_completed, badge_unlocked
- offline_mode_used, sync_completed

### **14) Asset Inventory (Delivery Checklist)**

- Logo (SVG/PNG), app icons (iOS/Android)
- Color & type tokens (Figma styles)
- Social templates (Reel covers, tiles)
